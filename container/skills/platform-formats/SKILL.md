# Platform Formats Skill

This skill contains the formatting rules, character limits, and best practices for each platform the Content Repurposing rig targets. Platform Writers and the Repurposing Strategist reference this to ensure every piece fits its destination.

## X/Twitter

### Format Rules
- **Character limit**: 280 per tweet
- **Thread format**: Numbered tweets (1/, 2/, etc.) or connected via reply chain
- **Hook tweet**: Must stand alone. Bold claim, surprising stat, or contrarian take. No hashtags on the hook.
- **Thread length**: 5-10 tweets. Under 5 feels thin. Over 10 loses attention.
- **Hashtags**: 0-2, only in the final tweet. Hashtag-heavy threads look spammy.
- **Links**: Only in the final tweet (or a reply below the thread). Links in the hook tank reach.
- **Media**: Threads with an image on tweet 1 get ~2x engagement. Suggest image use.
- **Line breaks**: Use sparingly within tweets. Each tweet should be one punchy unit.

### What Works
- Numbered frameworks ("7 ways to...", "3 mistakes that...")
- Contrarian takes that challenge conventional wisdom
- Personal stories with a lesson
- Data/stats that surprise
- "Thread:" or "A thread on..." as the hook signal (optional but common)

### What Doesn't Work
- Long paragraphs crammed into one tweet
- Hashtag spam (#marketing #growth #success on every tweet)
- Self-promotional threads without value
- Threads that read like a blog post broken into random chunks

### Engagement Patterns
- Best performing: Contrarian takes, practical frameworks, personal failures
- Hook formats that work: "Most people think X. They're wrong.", "[Number] [things] I learned from [experience]", "Unpopular opinion:"
- CTA formats: "Follow me for more on [topic]", "RT this thread if you found it useful", "What would you add?"

## LinkedIn

### Format Rules
- **Character limit**: 3,000 characters (but aim for 1,000-1,500 for engagement)
- **"See more" truncation**: First ~2 lines show before truncation. Hook MUST land here.
- **Paragraph length**: 1-3 sentences max. LinkedIn rewards white space.
- **Line breaks**: Use blank lines between paragraphs. Dense text gets skipped.
- **Hashtags**: 3-5 at the bottom. No inline hashtags.
- **Links**: Put in comments (links in posts reduce reach by ~40%). Mention "link in comments" if needed.
- **Media**: Single image or document (carousel PDF) outperforms text-only.

### What Works
- Professional insights and lessons
- Career/business stories with vulnerability
- Hot takes on industry trends
- "I [did something]. Here's what happened." narrative format
- Listicle format with line breaks ("5 things I learned...")
- "Agree or disagree?" engagement hooks

### What Doesn't Work
- Overly casual or slang-heavy content
- Clickbait without substance
- Posting links directly (kills reach)
- "Broetry" (one-word-per-line posts) -- this format is overused and mocked

### Voice Adaptation
- Shift toward professional but not corporate
- Keep personality -- LinkedIn rewards authenticity
- First person preferred over third person
- OK to be opinionated -- measured controversy drives engagement

## Instagram

### Carousel Format Rules
- **Slides**: 8-10 is the sweet spot (Instagram favors multi-image posts)
- **Slide 1 (Cover)**: Bold headline, 5-8 words max. Must stop the scroll. Use contrasting colors.
- **Slide 2-9**: One idea per slide. Short headline + 2-3 sentences or bullet points.
- **Final slide**: Clear CTA (Save, Share, Follow, Comment)
- **Text size**: Must be readable on mobile without zooming
- **Design**: Clean, consistent style across all slides. Use brand colors.

### Caption Rules
- **Length**: Up to 2,200 characters. Best engagement at 150-300 characters OR 1,000+ (bimodal)
- **First line**: Must hook before truncation (appears in feed before "more")
- **Hashtags**: 15-20 relevant hashtags. Mix broad (#marketing, 1M+ posts) with niche (#contentrepurposing, 10K-100K posts)
- **CTA**: "Save this for later", "Share with someone who needs this", "Double tap if you agree"
- **Emoji**: Acceptable on Instagram. Use sparingly for visual breaks, not decoration.

### What Works
- Step-by-step frameworks in carousel format
- Before/after contrasts
- "Swipe to learn" hooks on cover slide
- Educational content with visual appeal
- Personal stories in caption with visual quotes on slides

## Email Newsletter

### Format Rules
- **Subject line**: 40-60 characters. Must create curiosity or promise value.
- **Preview text**: 90-140 characters. Does NOT repeat the subject line. Adds context or intrigue.
- **Length**: 400-800 words for most newsletters. Can go to 1,200 for deep-dive issues.
- **Structure**: Personal greeting > Hook > Core content (1-3 sections) > CTA > Sign-off
- **Links**: 1-3 max. More than 3 feels like a link dump.
- **CTA**: One primary action per email. Reply, click, share, or implement.

### What Works
- Personal, conversational tone (subscriber feels like they're getting a letter from a friend)
- Opening with a personal story or observation
- One big idea per issue (not a roundup)
- "Reply to this email" CTAs (builds relationship, improves deliverability)
- PS: line for secondary CTA or personal note

### Subject Line Patterns
- Question: "Are you making this mistake?"
- Number: "3 things I changed this week"
- Curiosity gap: "I almost quit [thing]"
- Direct benefit: "How to [get result] without [pain]"
- Personal: "A confession about [topic]"

## Blog Post

### Format Rules
- **Title**: 50-65 characters. Include target keyword. Use power words.
- **Meta description**: 150-160 characters. Includes target keyword. Compelling summary.
- **Length**: 800-1,200 words (for repurposed content). Original long-form: 1,500-3,000.
- **Structure**: H1 (title) > Introduction > H2 sections (3-4) > Conclusion
- **Paragraphs**: 2-4 sentences. Break up walls of text.
- **Images**: Suggest 2-3 image placements (featured image, in-content, conclusion)
- **Internal links**: 2-3 links to related content (if client has it)
- **External links**: 1-2 authoritative sources for credibility

### SEO Essentials
- Target keyword in: title, first paragraph, one H2, meta description, last paragraph
- Use semantic variations naturally throughout (don't stuff)
- Include a question-based H2 for featured snippet potential
- Add alt text suggestions for images

### What Works
- Comprehensive coverage of a single topic
- Actionable takeaways (not just theory)
- Data and examples to support claims
- "How to" and "Why" formats
- Scannable format with bold key points

## Short-Form Video (YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels)

### Format Rules
- **Duration**: 30-60 seconds max. Under 45 seconds is ideal.
- **Hook**: First 3 seconds must grab. If they scroll, it's over.
- **Aspect ratio**: 9:16 (vertical)
- **Text overlays**: Key phrases on screen. Many watch muted.
- **Captions**: Always include burned-in captions
- **Music**: Optional -- talking head content often works without
- **CTA**: Final 5 seconds. Follow, comment, or "Part 2?"

### Script Structure
```
HOOK (0-3s): Bold statement, question, or surprising claim
CONTEXT (3-10s): Quick setup -- why this matters
CONTENT (10-45s): The insight, tip, story, or framework
CTA (45-60s): What to do next
```

### Hook Formats That Work
- "Stop doing [common thing]"
- "[Number] seconds to learn [valuable thing]"
- "Nobody talks about this but..."
- "Here's why [common belief] is wrong"
- "The #1 mistake [audience] makes"

### What Doesn't Work
- Slow intros ("Hey guys, so today I wanted to talk about...")
- Reading from a script with no energy
- No text overlays (50%+ watch muted)
- Over 60 seconds without exceptional content

## Pull Quotes / Social Graphics

### Format Rules
- **Quote length**: 5-25 words. Under 10 for maximum impact.
- **Attribution**: Always include the creator's name
- **Formats**: Square (1080x1080) for Instagram, Wide (1200x675) for X/LinkedIn
- **Design**: High contrast. Large text. Brand colors. Minimal decoration.
- **Types**: Insight quotes, hot takes, advice, personal reflections

### Selection Criteria
- Would someone screenshot this and share it? If no, skip it.
- Does it make sense without context? If no, skip it.
- Is it specific enough to be memorable? Generic inspiration quotes don't cut it.

## Audiogram Highlights

### Format Rules
- **Duration**: 30-60 seconds per highlight. Under 15 seconds for quick clips.
- **Transcript**: Exact words from the source. No paraphrasing.
- **Selection criteria**: Energy, insight, humor, emotion, or surprise
- **Format**: Audio waveform + text overlay + transcript animation
- **Tools**: Headliner, Descript, Podcast.co, or similar

### What Makes a Good Audiogram
- A moment where the speaker's energy noticeably peaks
- A counterintuitive insight that makes the listener think
- A funny or surprising moment
- A personal revelation or vulnerable admission
- A clear, quotable soundbite that works without context

# Google & Meta Ads Skill

Strategy, copy, targeting, and optimization for Google Search/Performance Max and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) campaigns.

---

## Google Ads

### Campaign Types & When to Use

| Type | Use when |
|------|----------|
| **Search** | Intent is high, people are actively searching for your solution |
| **Performance Max** | You have conversion data and want Google to optimize across all placements |
| **Demand Gen** | Building awareness on YouTube, Gmail, Discover |
| **Shopping** | E-commerce with product feed |

### Search Campaign Structure (Best Practice)

```
Campaign (1 per product/service/goal)
  └── Ad Group (1 theme per ad group)
        └── 3-5 keywords (same intent)
        └── 1 RSA (Responsive Search Ad)
        └── Ad extensions (sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets)
```

**Match type strategy:**
- Start with **Broad Match** + Smart Bidding (Target CPA/ROAS) — Google's algorithm needs data
- Use **Phrase Match** for branded and high-value terms you want control over
- Reserve **Exact Match** for your absolute best-performing terms

### RSA Copy Formula

**Headlines (15 options, 30 chars max each):**
- 3x keyword-focused ("Digital Marketing Agency Oslo")
- 3x benefit-focused ("Get More Leads In 30 Days")
- 3x social proof ("Trusted by 200+ Norwegian Businesses")
- 3x CTA ("Book a Free Strategy Call")
- 3x unique differentiator ("No Long-Term Contracts")

**Descriptions (4 options, 90 chars max):**
- Lead with the strongest benefit
- Include a number or specific claim
- End with CTA

### Bidding Strategy Guide

| Goal | Strategy |
|------|----------|
| First 2-4 weeks (no data) | Maximize Clicks with bid cap |
| 30+ conversions/month | Target CPA |
| E-commerce with ROAS data | Target ROAS |
| Brand awareness | Target Impression Share |

### Negative Keywords (Always Add These)

```
free, cheap, diy, how to, tutorial, course, training,
jobs, career, salary, wikipedia, reddit, youtube
+ all competitor brand names (unless running competitor campaigns)
```

## Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

### Campaign Structure

```
Campaign (objective: Leads / Sales / Awareness)
  └── Ad Set (audience + budget + placement)
        └── Ad (creative + copy)
```

**Best practice:** 1 campaign per objective, 2-4 ad sets (different audiences), 2-3 ads per ad set.

### Audience Strategy

**Cold traffic (new customers):**
- Broad targeting (age/gender/location only) — let Meta's algorithm find buyers
- Interest stacks (3-5 related interests per ad set)
- Lookalike audiences (1-3% of your buyer list)

**Warm traffic (retargeting):**
- Website visitors (last 30/60/90 days)
- Video viewers (50%+, 95%+)
- Instagram/Facebook engagers (last 60 days)
- Abandoned cart (last 14 days)

### Ad Copy Structure

```
HOOK LINE (first 1-2 lines before "see more")
↓
BODY (problem → solution → proof)
↓
CTA (single action)
↓
HEADLINE (in the link preview — separate from body copy)
↓
DESCRIPTION (1 line under headline)
```

**Hook line formulas:**
- "Stop [doing painful thing]."
- "[Number] [target audience] already [got result]."
- "The truth about [topic nobody talks about]."
- "If you're a [target] and you're struggling with [pain], read this."

### Creative Best Practices

| Format | When to use |
|--------|------------|
| **UGC-style video** | Cold traffic, trust-building, product demos |
| **Static image** | Retargeting, simple offers, fast A/B testing |
| **Carousel** | Multiple products, step-by-step, before/after |
| **Reel/Short video** | Awareness, viral potential, low CPM |

**Video structure (30 sec):**
1. Hook (0-3 sec) — stop the scroll
2. Problem (3-8 sec) — make them feel seen
3. Solution/demo (8-22 sec) — show, don't just tell
4. CTA (22-30 sec) — one clear action

## Performance Analysis

### Google — Key Metrics to Watch

| Metric | Healthy range | Red flag |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| CTR (Search) | 5-10%+ | Below 3% |
| Quality Score | 7-10 | Below 5 |
| Conv. Rate | Depends on industry | Dropping week-over-week |
| Impression Share | 60%+ for brand | Below 40% |
| CPA | Below target | 2x+ target CPA |

### Meta — Key Metrics to Watch

| Metric | Healthy range | Red flag |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| CTR (link) | 1-3%+ | Below 0.5% |
| CPM | Varies by audience | Rising 20%+ week-over-week |
| Frequency | 1.5-3 (cold) | Above 4 = ad fatigue |
| ROAS | Above 2x for profitability | Below breakeven ROAS |
| Hook Rate | 25%+ (video) | Below 15% = change hook |

## Troubleshooting

**Low CTR on Google Search:**
→ Rewrite headlines. Add more specific benefits. Check keyword → ad relevance.

**High CPC on Meta:**
→ Audience too narrow, or ad fatigue (check frequency). Broaden targeting or refresh creative.

**Campaigns spending but no conversions:**
→ Check landing page (load speed, mobile, CTA clarity). Check conversion tracking. Check audience-offer match.

**Campaigns not spending:**
→ Bid too low, audience too narrow, budget too low, or ad in review/rejected.

## Output Format

When auditing campaigns, deliver:
1. **What's working** (don't fix what isn't broken)
2. **Top 3 issues** with specific fix for each
3. **Quick wins** (changes to make this week)
4. **Long-term recommendations** (structural changes)

# Platform Strategy Skill

This skill provides platform-specific guidance for building content strategies, templates, and calendars. Each platform has its own culture, algorithm, and audience expectations. The strategy must account for these differences.

## X/Twitter Strategy

### Algorithm Priorities (2025-2026)
- Replies and conversations weighted heavily
- Long-form posts (Notes) getting more distribution
- Threads still perform well for authority building
- Media (images, video) boosts reach
- Link posts get suppressed (put links in replies)

### Content Strategy for X
- **Primary use**: Thought leadership, networking, real-time engagement
- **Best content types**: Threads, hot takes, numbered frameworks, reply engagement
- **Posting frequency**: 1-2/day minimum for growth
- **Voice**: Most direct, opinionated version of the brand
- **Growth levers**: Threads, quote tweets with insight, reply networking

### Template Categories
1. **Thread templates**: Hook patterns, framework structures, story arcs
2. **Single tweet templates**: Hot takes, one-liners, questions
3. **Engagement templates**: Reply frameworks, quote tweet patterns
4. **Promotional templates**: Soft sell, CTA placement

### Thread Template Structures

**The Framework Thread** (5-8 tweets):
1. Hook: Bold claim or surprising stat
2. Context: Why this matters
3-6. Framework steps (one per tweet)
7. Summary/takeaway
8. CTA

**The Story Thread** (5-10 tweets):
1. Hook: "Last week I [did something]. Here's what happened."
2. Setup: The situation
3-6. The journey (what happened, what went wrong, what worked)
7-8. The lesson/insight
9. Application for the reader
10. CTA

**The Contrarian Thread** (5-7 tweets):
1. Hook: "Unpopular opinion: [conventional wisdom] is wrong"
2. Why people believe it
3-4. Why it's wrong (evidence)
5-6. What to do instead
7. CTA

## LinkedIn Strategy

### Algorithm Priorities (2025-2026)
- Dwell time (how long people spend reading your post)
- Comments weighted 5-10x more than likes
- Early engagement (first 60 minutes) determines distribution
- Document posts (carousels) get extended reach
- Links in posts get suppressed (put in comments)

### Content Strategy for LinkedIn
- **Primary use**: Authority building, lead generation, professional networking
- **Best content types**: Narrative posts, listicles, document carousels, polls
- **Posting frequency**: 3-5/week for growth
- **Voice**: Professional but personal, thought-leader mode
- **Growth levers**: Comments on others' posts, document/carousel posts, story-driven content

### Template Categories
1. **Post templates**: Story posts, listicle posts, hot take posts, lesson posts
2. **Carousel templates**: Framework carousels, how-to carousels
3. **Comment templates**: Networking comment frameworks
4. **Article templates**: Long-form LinkedIn article structures

### Post Template Structures

**The Lesson Post**:
```
[Hook: Bold opening that stops the scroll -- 2 lines max before "see more"]

[Setup: What happened -- 2-3 short paragraphs]

[The lesson: What I learned -- 1-2 paragraphs]

[The takeaway: What the reader should do -- 1 paragraph]

[CTA: Question to drive comments]

[3-5 hashtags]
```

**The Listicle Post**:
```
[Hook: "[Number] things I learned from [experience]"]

1. [Point] -- [one-line explanation]
2. [Point] -- [one-line explanation]
[Continue]

[Summary/callout]

[CTA]

[Hashtags]
```

## Instagram Strategy

### Algorithm Priorities (2025-2026)
- Saves and shares weighted more than likes
- Reels dominate discovery
- Carousels get extended distribution (shown multiple times)
- Consistent posting rewarded (minimum 3/week)
- Story engagement affects feed ranking

### Content Strategy for Instagram
- **Primary use**: Brand awareness, visual authority, community building
- **Best content types**: Carousels, Reels, Stories, single-image quotes
- **Posting frequency**: 3-5/week feed, daily Stories
- **Voice**: Most warm, visual, accessible version of the brand
- **Growth levers**: Carousels (saves), Reels (discovery), Stories (engagement)

### Template Categories
1. **Carousel templates**: Educational, framework, before/after, myth-busting
2. **Caption templates**: Story captions, educational captions, CTA captions
3. **Reel script templates**: Hook-content-CTA structure
4. **Story templates**: Poll, question, behind-the-scenes

### Carousel Template Structures

**The Educational Carousel** (8-10 slides):
1. Cover: Bold headline (5-8 words)
2. Problem/question
3-8. Solution steps or points (one per slide)
9. Summary/recap
10. CTA slide (save/share/follow)

## Email Strategy

### Best Practices (2025-2026)
- Mobile-first design (60%+ open on mobile)
- Personalization beyond first name
- Segmentation by engagement level
- Plain text outperforms heavy HTML for newsletters
- Reply-to engagement builds deliverability

### Content Strategy for Email
- **Primary use**: Relationship building, conversion, retention
- **Best content types**: Newsletters, nurture sequences, promotional emails, re-engagement
- **Sending frequency**: 1-2/week for newsletters, drip for sequences
- **Voice**: Most intimate, personal version of the brand
- **Growth levers**: Lead magnets, referral programs, content upgrades

### Template Categories
1. **Newsletter templates**: Weekly insight, roundup, deep-dive
2. **Welcome sequence templates**: 3-5 email onboarding flows
3. **Nurture sequence templates**: Problem-agitation-solution arcs
4. **Promotional templates**: Launch, offer, deadline

### Newsletter Template Structure
```
Subject: [40-60 chars, creates curiosity or promises value]
Preview: [90-140 chars, does NOT repeat subject]

[Personal greeting]

[Hook: Personal story or observation -- 2-3 sentences]

[Core insight: The main idea -- 2-3 paragraphs]

[Application: How the reader can use this -- 1-2 paragraphs]

[CTA: One clear action]

[Sign-off in brand voice]

PS: [Optional secondary CTA or personal note]
```

## Blog/SEO Strategy

### Best Practices (2025-2026)
- E-E-A-T signals matter more than ever (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
- Long-form (1,500-3,000 words) outperforms for organic search
- AI-generated content penalized unless it adds genuine value
- Featured snippets and People Also Ask optimization
- Internal linking architecture matters for topical authority

### Content Strategy for Blog
- **Primary use**: Organic search traffic, authority building, lead generation
- **Best content types**: How-to guides, comparison posts, ultimate guides, case studies
- **Publishing frequency**: 2-4/month for most businesses
- **Voice**: Most thorough, expert version of the brand
- **Growth levers**: Topical authority, internal linking, featured snippet optimization

### Template Categories
1. **How-to post template**: Step-by-step guide structure
2. **Comparison post template**: X vs Y structure
3. **Ultimate guide template**: Comprehensive topic coverage
4. **Case study template**: Problem-solution-results structure
5. **Listicle template**: "[Number] best [things] for [audience]"

## Outreach Strategy

### DM Scripts
- Platform-specific (LinkedIn DMs vs. Instagram DMs vs. X DMs)
- Value-first approach (share something useful, then introduce yourself)
- Short (3-5 sentences max)
- Specific to the recipient (reference their content)

### Partnership Pitches
- Mutual value proposition
- Specific collaboration idea (not vague "let's collaborate")
- Social proof (your audience size, past collaborations)
- Clear next step

### Collaboration Proposals
- Formal structure for larger partnerships
- Timeline, deliverables, and expectations
- Audience overlap data
- Content format proposal (podcast swap, joint webinar, co-created content)

## Template Quality Standards

Every template should include:
1. **Structure**: Clear sections with labeled placeholders
2. **Examples**: At least one filled-in example per template
3. **Customization notes**: What to change and what to keep
4. **Platform rules**: Character limits, formatting conventions
5. **Voice integration**: Notes on how to apply the brand voice to the template

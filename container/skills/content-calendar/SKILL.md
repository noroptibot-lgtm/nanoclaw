# Content Calendar Skill

This skill provides the methodology for building content calendars that are both strategic and actionable. A good calendar isn't a list of post ideas -- it's a system that ensures every piece of content connects to a business goal, varies in format and platform, and builds momentum over time.

## Core Principle

A content calendar has three layers:
1. **Strategic layer**: Monthly themes aligned to business goals and content pillars
2. **Tactical layer**: Weekly content batches with platform-specific assignments
3. **Execution layer**: Individual content briefs with hooks, formats, and publish dates

## Calendar Building Process

### Step 1: Map Monthly Themes

Each month gets a primary theme and a secondary theme:

**Month 1 themes** should focus on:
- Establishing authority in the content pillars
- Introducing the brand's perspective
- Building foundational content that can be referenced later

**Month 2 themes** should focus on:
- Going deeper on topics that resonated in Month 1
- Introducing engagement-focused content
- Building community and conversation

**Month 3 themes** should focus on:
- Conversion-focused content
- Social proof and case studies
- Clear CTAs and offers

### Step 2: Plan Weekly Content Batches

Each week follows a rhythm:

**The Anchor Content Model**:
- 1 **anchor piece** per week (long-form blog post, newsletter, or in-depth post)
- 3-5 **derivative pieces** per week (adapted from the anchor or complementary)
- 1-2 **engagement pieces** per week (questions, polls, behind-the-scenes)

**Weekly Content Batch Template**:
```
Week of [Date]:
  Theme: [Weekly theme within monthly theme]
  Anchor: [Blog post / Newsletter] -- [Topic]

  Monday: [Platform] -- [Content type] -- [Brief description]
  Tuesday: [Platform] -- [Content type] -- [Brief description]
  Wednesday: [Platform] -- [Content type] -- [Brief description]
  Thursday: [Platform] -- [Content type] -- [Brief description]
  Friday: [Platform] -- [Content type] -- [Brief description]

  Engagement: [Platform] -- [Engagement content for the week]
```

### Step 3: Assign Content Types

Vary content types across the week to prevent audience fatigue:

| Day Pattern | Content Type | Purpose |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| Anchor Day (Mon/Tue) | Long-form, educational | Value delivery, authority |
| Mid-week (Wed) | Story or behind-the-scenes | Connection, trust |
| Engagement Day (Thu) | Question, poll, discussion | Community building |
| Promotional (Fri) | Soft CTA, case study, offer | Conversion |
| Weekend (Sat/Sun) | Casual, personal, or skip | Authenticity (optional) |

### Step 4: Identify Anchor Content for Repurposing

Mark calendar items that are ideal repurposing candidates:

**Repurposing candidates** (mark with [REPURPOSE] tag):
- Blog posts with frameworks or numbered insights
- Newsletter issues with strong original commentary
- LinkedIn posts that could expand into blog posts
- Video content with quotable moments

**What makes good anchor content for repurposing**:
- Contains 3+ distinct ideas (each can become a standalone piece)
- Has quotable lines or surprising data
- Covers a topic the audience cares about across platforms
- Can be broken into multiple formats without losing value

### Step 5: Add Platform-Specific Timing

**Best posting times** (general guidelines, adjust based on data):

| Platform | Best Times | Best Days |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| X/Twitter | 8-10 AM, 12-1 PM, 5-6 PM | Tue-Thu |
| LinkedIn | 7-8 AM, 12 PM, 5-6 PM | Tue-Thu |
| Instagram | 11 AM-1 PM, 7-9 PM | Mon-Fri |
| Email | 6-8 AM, 10 AM | Tue-Thu |
| Blog | Anytime (SEO-driven, not time-sensitive) | Anytime |

## 30-Day Calendar Structure (Light Mode)

For light mode, produce a 30-day calendar with:
- 4 weekly batches
- 1 monthly theme
- Daily content assignments for primary platforms
- Anchor content marked for repurposing
- Content types and brief descriptions (not full briefs)

For full mode, produce a 90-day calendar with:
- 12 weekly batches (4 per month)
- 3 monthly themes with progression (foundation -> growth -> conversion)
- Daily content assignments for all platforms
- Anchor content marked for repurposing
- Content briefs for each item (2-3 sentence description with hook idea)
- Blog post schedule integrated
- Email sequence touchpoints

## Calendar Quality Standards

1. **Every item has a purpose**: No "post something on Instagram" -- every item has a topic, format, and pillar assignment.
2. **Platform variety**: No more than 2 consecutive days on the same platform without a different content type.
3. **Pillar balance**: All pillars represented roughly equally across the 30/90 days.
4. **Content type variety**: Mix educational, story, engagement, and promotional content across each week.
5. **Repurposing marked**: Every anchor piece that could be repurposed is tagged [REPURPOSE].
6. **Realistic frequency**: Calendar should be achievable by a solopreneur or small team. Don't assign 3 posts/day unless the client specifically can handle that volume.
7. **Theme progression**: Month-over-month themes should build on each other, not repeat.

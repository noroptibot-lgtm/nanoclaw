# Page CRO (Landing Page Conversion Optimization)

Optimize landing pages for maximum conversion. This skill focuses specifically on single-offer landing pages used in small business marketing funnels -- not homepages, pricing pages, or multi-purpose sites.

## The 7-Tier Optimization Hierarchy

Analyze and optimize in this order. Higher tiers have more impact.

### Tier 1: Value Proposition Clarity (Highest Impact)

The 5-second test: Can a visitor understand what this is and why they should care within 5 seconds of landing?

**Check for:**
- Headline communicates core benefit, not just what the product is
- Written in customer language, not company jargon
- Specific enough to be meaningful ("Cut your weekly reporting from 4 hours to 15 minutes" not "Save time")
- Differentiated from competitors (why THIS solution?)

**Common failures:**
- Feature-focused instead of benefit-focused
- Too clever (sacrificing clarity for wordplay)
- Trying to say everything instead of the most important thing
- Generic phrasing that could apply to any business in the category

### Tier 2: Headline Effectiveness

**Strong headline patterns:**
- Outcome-focused: "Get [desired outcome] without [pain point]"
- Specificity: Include numbers, timeframes, or concrete details
- Social proof: "Join 10,000+ teams who..."
- Question: Highlight the main pain point as a question
- Direct: State exactly what you offer and for whom

**Headline must match traffic source.** If the ad says "Free guide to doubling your bookings," the landing page headline must reference that guide and that outcome. Mismatch kills conversion.

### Tier 3: CTA Placement, Copy, and Hierarchy

**Primary CTA assessment:**
- One clear primary action per page
- Visible without scrolling (above the fold)
- Repeated at the bottom after the full argument is made
- Button copy communicates value, not just action

**CTA strength scale:**
- Weak: Submit, Sign Up, Learn More, Click Here, Get Started
- Strong: Get the Free Guide, Start My Trial, Download Now, Send Me the Checklist
- Formula: [Action Verb] + [What They Get] + [Qualifier]

**No competing CTAs.** A landing page has ONE thing it wants the visitor to do. Do not add navigation, sidebar links, footer links to other pages, or secondary CTAs that dilute focus.

### Tier 4: Visual Hierarchy and Scannability

- Most visitors scan, not read. The page must communicate its core message through headlines, subheadlines, and visual elements alone
- Bold key phrases within body text
- Use whitespace to separate sections
- Images should support the message (product screenshots, relevant photos) not decorate
- Icons and numbered lists improve scanability

### Tier 5: Trust Signals and Social Proof

**Types (in order of strength):**
1. Specific customer testimonials with name, title, company, photo
2. Case study snippets with real numbers ("Increased bookings by 47%")
3. Review scores and counts ("4.8 stars from 230+ reviews")
4. Customer logos (recognizable ones)
5. Certifications, awards, media mentions
6. Money-back guarantees, risk reversals

**Placement:** Near CTAs and immediately after benefit claims. Trust signals address the internal question "Can I trust this?"

**For small businesses without testimonials:** Use social proof alternatives -- number of customers served, years in business, professional certifications, before/after results, industry association memberships.

### Tier 6: Objection Handling

**Common objections for small business services:**
- "Is this worth the money?" -- Address with ROI framing, guarantee, payment plans
- "Will this work for MY business?" -- Address with case studies in their vertical
- "I don't have time for this." -- Address with done-for-you framing, time to results
- "I've tried something similar before." -- Address with differentiation, why this is different
- "I need to think about it." -- Address with urgency (limited spots, seasonal timing)

**FAQ section best practices:**
- Address real objections, not softballs
- Keep answers short (2-4 sentences)
- Use accordion/details elements (don't show all at once)
- Place FAQ after features, before final CTA

### Tier 7: Friction Points

- Form fields: Ask for the minimum (name + email for lead gen). Every extra field reduces conversion
- Page load speed: Under 3 seconds. Tailwind CDN loads fast; avoid heavy images
- Mobile experience: More than 60% of traffic is mobile. Test at 375px width
- Unclear next steps: After the CTA, what happens? Set expectations
- Broken elements: Missing images, placeholder text, non-functional forms

## Section Ordering

The proven high-conversion order for a single-offer landing page:

1. **Hero** -- Headline + subheadline + primary CTA + supporting text
2. **Problem** -- Show you understand their pain (builds empathy)
3. **Solution** -- Present your offer as the answer to their problem
4. **Social Proof** -- Testimonials, case studies, results
5. **Features / What You Get** -- Specific deliverables or benefits
6. **FAQ** -- Handle objections
7. **Final CTA** -- Recap value proposition + signup form

This order follows the psychological sequence: Attention > Problem awareness > Solution awareness > Trust > Details > Objection resolution > Action.

## Mobile-First Requirements

More than half of landing page visitors are on mobile. These are non-negotiable:

**Typography:**
- Body text: minimum 16px (1rem)
- Headlines: minimum 24px (1.5rem) on mobile, scale up on desktop
- Line height: 1.5-1.75 for body text

**Touch targets:**
- Buttons: minimum 44px height, full-width on mobile
- Form inputs: minimum 44px height, generous padding
- Links: sufficient spacing between clickable elements

**Layout:**
- Single column on mobile, expand to 2-3 columns on md: breakpoint
- Horizontal padding: minimum 24px (px-6) on mobile
- Section padding: py-16 minimum for breathing room

**Forms:**
- Stack fields vertically on mobile
- Input labels or clear placeholders
- Large submit button (full width on mobile)

**Images:**
- Lazy load any images below the fold
- Use appropriate sizes (don't load desktop images on mobile)
- Test that images don't push content below the fold

## Conversion Benchmarks by Vertical

These are typical landing page conversion rates for small business funnels. Use as reference points, not guarantees.

| Vertical | Lead Gen (email) | Paid Lead Gen | Notes |
|----------|-----------------|---------------|-------|
| Local services (plumbers, dentists, etc.) | 8-15% | 5-10% | High intent, specific need |
| Fitness / Gyms | 10-20% | 6-12% | Free trial/class offers convert well |
| Coaches / Consultants | 5-12% | 3-8% | Trust signals critical |
| Restaurants / Food | 12-20% | 8-15% | Low commitment offers work |
| Real estate | 3-8% | 2-5% | Higher consideration purchase |
| E-commerce | 2-5% | 1-3% | Direct purchase, higher friction |
| SaaS / Software | 3-8% | 2-5% | Free trial lowers barrier |

**Lead magnet offers** (free guide, checklist, quiz) typically convert 2-3x higher than direct sales offers on cold traffic.

## Funnel Flow: Page-to-Page Continuity

The landing page is not standalone. It connects to the thank-you page, which connects to the email sequence. Continuity matters:

**Landing page > Thank-you page:**
- Thank-you page confirms what they signed up for (match the language)
- Deliver the lead magnet immediately
- Set expectations for what comes next ("Check your inbox for...")
- Do NOT hard-sell on the thank-you page

**Landing page > Email sequence:**
- Email 1 must reference the exact lead magnet and landing page promise
- The offer in the final email should feel like a natural extension of the landing page value
- Voice and tone must match across all pieces

**Landing page > Ad copy:**
- The ad promise must be fulfilled on the landing page
- Headline should echo or directly match the ad's hook
- If the ad promises "5 tips to double your bookings," the landing page must be about those 5 tips

## Quality Review Checklist

Use this when reviewing a completed landing page:

- [ ] Headline passes the 5-second test (clear value, not generic)
- [ ] Problem > Solution > Proof > CTA flow is present
- [ ] Copy is specific to this business (not template-sounding)
- [ ] Single clear CTA (no competing actions, no navigation)
- [ ] Social proof section present with specific details
- [ ] FAQ addresses real objections (not softballs)
- [ ] Form asks for minimum fields (name + email)
- [ ] Mobile responsive (tested at 375px width)
- [ ] Page loads without errors
- [ ] Thank-you page link works
- [ ] Message matches between ad, landing page, and thank-you page

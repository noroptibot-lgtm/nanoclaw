# Compound words (sammensatte ord) and særskriving

The single most common Norwegian writing mistake, even by natives, is **særskriving**: splitting a compound word into two. This is wrong and changes meaning.

## The rule

In Norwegian, when two nouns combine into one concept, they are written as **one word**. No space. No hyphen (with rare exceptions).

## Classic examples (wrong → right)

| Wrong (særskriving) | Right | English |
|---|---|---|
| lamme lår | lammelår | leg of lamb |
| røyk fritt | røykfritt | smoke-free |
| kunde service | kundeservice | customer service |
| sykkel hjelm | sykkelhjelm | bike helmet |
| barne hage | barnehage | kindergarten |
| jule bord | julebord | Christmas dinner (work) |
| vei arbeid | veiarbeid | road work |
| stor salg | storsalg | big sale |
| god morgen kaffe | godmorgenkaffe | morning coffee |
| netto butikk | nettbutikk | online shop |
| nett side | nettside | website |
| markeds føring | markedsføring | marketing |
| sales tjeneste | salgstjeneste | sales service |
| kunde behandler | kundebehandler | customer rep |
| pris liste | prisliste | price list |
| leverings tid | leveringstid | delivery time |
| betalings betingelser | betalingsbetingelser | payment terms |
| bygge tjenester | byggetjenester | construction services |
| rengjørings firma | rengjøringsfirma | cleaning company |
| flytte byrå | flyttebyrå | moving company |

## Why særskriving is bad

It changes meaning. "Lamme lår" means "paralysed thighs". "Lammelår" means "leg of lamb". A native reader notices instantly and judges the writer as careless or foreign.

## Marketing words to ALWAYS check

These come up constantly in Albert's work and are nearly always written as one word:

- nettside (not nett side)
- nettbutikk (not nett butikk)
- markedsføring (not markeds føring)
- annonsekampanje (not annonse kampanje)
- kundereise (not kunde reise)
- leadgenerering (one word, loanword OK)
- konverteringsrate (not konverterings rate)
- målgruppe (not mål gruppe)
- annonsetekst (not annonse tekst)
- landingsside (not landings side)
- kampanjebudsjett (not kampanje budsjett)

## When in doubt

Three checks:
1. Can you say the two parts as one stress unit? Then it is one word.
2. Does the meaning change if you split it? Then it is one word.
3. Would Google return more results for the joined version? It is one word.

When still unsure, write the joined version. Særskriving is the bigger error than over-joining.

## Hyphens (rare)

Use hyphens only for:
- Combining a number and a word: "5-årsdag", "10-års jubileum"
- Avoiding triple letters that look weird (modern usage allows both, prefer joined)
- Two proper nouns: "Oslo-mannen", "Bergen-toget"
- Abbreviation + word: "VVS-firma", "PR-byrå", "SMS-kampanje"

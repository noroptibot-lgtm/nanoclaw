# Newsletter Copy Skill

Write newsletters and email sequences that get opened, read, and clicked — using direct-response copywriting psychology.

---

## Core Frameworks

### AIDA (Awareness → Interest → Desire → Action)
- **Awareness:** Subject line + first sentence — ONE job: get the open
- **Interest:** Hook the reader in sentence 2-3 with a bold claim or story
- **Desire:** Build the case — proof, specifics, what they get
- **Action:** Single clear CTA. One link. One ask.

### PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solution)
Best for sales emails and promotional sends.
1. **Problem:** Name the pain they already feel
2. **Agitate:** Make it worse. Twist the knife.
3. **Solution:** Position your offer as the obvious exit

### Open Loop
Start with an incomplete story or unanswered question. Force the read.
> "Last Tuesday I made a mistake that cost me 3 clients. Here's what I did wrong — and how I fixed it."

## Subject Line Formula

The subject line has ONE job: get the open. It should:
- Be under 50 characters (mobile preview)
- Create a curiosity gap OR make a specific promise
- Never reveal the full value — make them open to get it

**High-performing structures:**
| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| Curiosity gap | "You're probably doing this wrong" |
| Specific benefit | "3 lines that doubled our reply rate" |
| Negative curiosity | "Why I stopped using [common tactic]" |
| Story tease | "I fired my best client last week" |
| Number | "7 things I wish I knew in year 1" |

**Avoid:**
- "Newsletter #42"
- "Check out our latest update"
- All caps or excessive punctuation

## Email Structure (Sales Email)

```
SUBJECT: [curiosity gap or specific promise]

OPENING LINE: Bold statement, story hook, or surprising fact.
(This is your scroll-stopper. 1-2 sentences max.)

BODY: 3-5 short paragraphs.
- Name the problem/context
- Build the desire (proof, specifics, transformation)
- Handle the objection (preempt the "but...")

CTA: One link. One ask. Make it obvious.
"[Click here to ___]" or "Reply with ___ and I'll send you ___"

SIGN-OFF: Personal. Name only. No "Best regards."
```

## Psychology Triggers (Use Sparingly)

| Trigger | How to use |
|---------|-----------|
| **Social proof** | Specific numbers: "412 people signed up in 48 hours" |
| **Scarcity** | Real limits only: "Closing Friday at midnight" |
| **Authority** | Drop credentials naturally in the story, not in the bio |
| **Specificity** | "Generated €47,382" beats "made a lot of money" |
| **Loss aversion** | "What you're leaving on the table" > "what you could gain" |

## Newsletter vs Sales Email

| | Newsletter | Sales Email |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Build trust, stay top of mind | Convert to action |
| CTA | Soft ("hit reply", "share if useful") | Hard ("buy", "book", "sign up") |
| Length | 300-600 words | 150-350 words |
| Frequency | 1-3x/week | Campaign-based |
| Tone | Conversational, story-led | Direct, benefit-led |

## Sequence Structure (5-Email Welcome Sequence)

1. **Email 1 — Welcome + deliver promise** (sent immediately)
   - Thank them, deliver what you promised, set expectations
2. **Email 2 — Your story** (day 2)
   - Why you do what you do. Build trust through vulnerability.
3. **Email 3 — Quick win** (day 4)
   - Give them something immediately useful. Prove your value.
4. **Email 4 — Social proof** (day 6)
   - Case study or testimonial. Make the transformation real.
5. **Email 5 — Soft offer** (day 8)
   - Introduce your offer. Low pressure. "If you want to go deeper..."

## Writing Rules

- **Short paragraphs.** 1-3 sentences max. White space = readability.
- **Active voice.** "The campaign generated 40 leads" not "40 leads were generated."
- **One idea per email.** If you have two ideas, write two emails.
- **Read it out loud.** If it sounds stiff, rewrite it.
- **Cut the preamble.** Delete the first paragraph. Most emails start too slow.
- **Never bury the CTA.** If it's important, say it early AND at the end.

---

## Output Format

When writing email copy, always deliver:
1. **Subject line** (3 options — let the user pick)
2. **Preview text** (40-90 chars, complements subject line)
3. **Full email body**
4. **CTA text**
5. **Brief note** on which psychological trigger is doing the work
